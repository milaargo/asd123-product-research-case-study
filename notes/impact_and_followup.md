# Impact and Follow-Up

## Product Launch

The redesigned ASD123 experience launched in March 2026.

Two recommendations directly informed by the research were implemented:

- the activity roadmap;
- the rewards system.

## Founder Follow-Up

I later interviewed co-founder Sheeva to understand what had happened after the original project.

She said the decision to look beyond clinicians and examine the broader learning ecosystem stood out in the original research.

She described the work as creating a "ripple effect" across their understanding of:

- what children were interested in;
- how they learned;
- what shaped their experience outside therapy.

The research also reinforced directions the team had been considering, including onboarding and a stronger parent experience.

Ultimately, the roadmap and rewards system were selected for implementation.

## Qualitative Post-Launch Signals

Sheeva shared several pieces of qualitative feedback:

> "The research helped bridge the therapist side and parent/child side in a seamless way."

> "One child in particular started reaching for the phone to play our games rather than her toys."

> "Feedback I hear constantly from parents is how easy the roadmap is to maneuver."

These observations are useful directional evidence, but they are not controlled measures of product impact.
